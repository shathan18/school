Final: subject-aware scorer — the fix does NOT work

Bottom line: the multiplicative reformulation removed the degenerate blank-wall optimum (a real bug fix), so the hypothesis finally got a fair test — and it failed. Penalising off-subject shadow nudges bad cross-talk down slightly but does not make it reliably low. The seed still dominates.

The degeneracy that was fixed (worth keeping)

The earlier additive penalty gain − λ·spill was broken by construction: a panel aimed at nothing scores 0 (no gain, no spill), while any useful panel necessarily spills onto background and goes negative once λ·spill > gain. So 0 beat negative and the greedy picked 14 panels that missed the wall entirely — 0% bad cross-talk on every seed because the walls were blank (image-completeness 0.0%). The multiplicative form gain·(1−spill_frac)^λ cannot do this: a zero-gain panel scores 0 and can never beat a real one. Min completeness across all 40 configs: 64.8% (was 0.0%).

Correction to my own harness

The DISQUALIFIED (blank wall) label in the per-seed table below is a misnomer — those configs are not blank (65–93% completeness), they just miss the 85% bar, which disqualified 6/10 of even the control. That shrank guard-passing samples to 1–4 seeds and made those sub-summaries statistically worthless. The all-seed table above is the honest comparison. Flagging this rather than letting the label imply the degeneracy persisted.

Honest final state for this cycle

  • Bad cross-talk is seed-sensitive (~14–37%) and no lever tested reliably fixes it.
  • Density: ruled out — flat 21–24% regardless of shard count. And on the real pipeline more shards makes structure worse (older ~220 finding vindicated; structural optimum is far below the ceiling, ~55/29 shards).
  • Wide-angle diversity: direction real, specific numbers inside the noise band (the 11.0% I reported was noise — 14.4% on a 0.5% parameter change).
  • Subject-aware scorer penalty: −3 points mean, tighter variance, but not reliably low. Insufficient.

The presentable result is the diagnosis: cross-talk is the dominant noise source, it is a placement problem, and it is neither a density nor a shard-count problem — plus the honest limitation that placement-scorer penalties alone do not solve it.
