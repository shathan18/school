The best SSIM here doesn't mean the best reconstruction. A high-key image is mostly bright, low-variance pixels, and SSIM scores those generously — a faint reconstruction of a faint target lands high without carrying much. Two honest notes

Smooth beats blotchy

Where the high-contrast run clipped shards to pure black/white and looked scattered, high key keeps everything in a narrow bright band, so shard tones sit close together and the surface reads smooth. If you want a gentle, delicate object, this is the tonal end to aim for — at the cost of presence.

Faint, and still soft

Little dark content means little to reconstruct — the figure reads as a ghost. And as in every girl run the face doesn't resolve; that's the shard-count wall, untouched by tone. Grayscale is still a K-sheet-only monochrome build.
