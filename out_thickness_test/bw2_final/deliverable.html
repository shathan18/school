Two honest notes

Contrast is not the graphic lever

What actually helps the medium is turning an image into bold flat shapes (posterising to a few solid tones). Cranking contrast on a photo keeps the fine tonal detail and just clips it — so you get harsher blocks, not clearer shapes. Prefer posterise over contrast if the goal is graphic legibility.

Still soft, still black-only

As with every girl run, the face doesn't resolve (a shard-count limit, unchanged by tone), and grayscale still collapses the build to the K sheet alone — a simpler monochrome object that forfeits the coloured-shard idea.
