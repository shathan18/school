How the panel configuration is derived

The layout is not hand-designed. It is the output of a greedy search that adds one panel at a time — each panel chosen, from 16 random candidates, to cover the most image content the previous panels left missing. You can watch the two target images fill in as panels are added.

The declining score is the natural stopping signal: when the next panel adds almost nothing, stop. That is how panel count could become an emergent result rather than a chosen number.
