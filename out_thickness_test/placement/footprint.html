How we know where to place the panels

We do not place the panels "in" the pictures. The panels float in the room corner. What we place them by is where their shadow lands on the picture. Each candidate panel casts a shadow onto each wall (bright = the picture content that shadow actually covers; faded = the rest). How much picture content the shadow covers IS the panel's score, and the search keeps the best.

Notice each panel covers a different region of each picture, and the shadow regions are slanted/keystoned because the panels are tilted. The search picks panels one at a time, each chosen to cover the picture content the earlier panels missed.
