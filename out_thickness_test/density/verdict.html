REAL-pipeline density sweep — the "more shards = cleaner" claim does NOT hold

Fixed base layout (wide-angle, seed 3, scorer unchanged), cross-talk present, fabrication budget disabled so shard count actually crosses the ~220/wall ceiling. Density is the only variable.

Headline: tonight's earlier "more shards = cleaner" was an artifact of deleting cross-talk. It reverses on the real pipeline.

1. Does fidelity keep improving with density? — No. It splits by metric.

  • RMSE (colour error) keeps improving slightly with more shards: Wall A 0.235 → 0.194.
  • SSIM (structure) PEAKS at 0.5× and then declines monotonically: Wall A 0.796 → 0.709; Wall B 0.813 → 0.710 across 0.5×→4×.
  • Edge-fidelity also peaks at 0.5× (A 0.561, B 0.364) and never recovers.

This is exactly the signature the older project finding describes: more shard regions lower SSIM/edge-fidelity even as average colour error improves slightly, because each extra region is another independent colour + host-panel decision adding boundary noise faster than it resolves detail. The old finding is vindicated on the real pipeline.

2. Where does it turn? — Far BELOW the 220 ceiling, not at it.

Structural fidelity peaks at 0.5× = 55 (A) / 29 (B) shards per wall — roughly 0.25× of the 220 ceiling — and degrades from 1× onward. So the ~220 number was never the fidelity optimum; it is a fabrication upper bound (what's buildable), and the project docs are consistent in calling it "a ceiling, not a target to inflate toward." The real structural sweet spot for this layout is ~4× lower than the ceiling. Pushing to 3-4× (315-394 shards, 1.4-1.8× over the ceiling) is worse on every structural metric AND unbuildable.

3. Does bad cross-talk change with density? — Essentially no. It's a placement problem.

Wall B bad cross-talk: 31.6% (0.25×), 14.4% (0.5×), 21.5% (1×), 21.8% (2×), 24.0% (3×), 22.4% (4×). Across 1×→4× the shard count rises 4× while bad cross-talk stays flat at ~21-24%. It is density-independent — you cannot tune it away with shard count. This confirms the scorer-fix direction is the right one regardless of density.

Correction to my previous message: I reported wide/0.5× hitting B_bad = 11.0%. The same nominal config here (fragment_size 0.190 vs 0.1909 — a 0.5% difference) gives 14.4%. That swing on a negligible parameter change means B_bad has high variance at low shard counts, so the "under 15%" result is not robust. I should not have presented it as a solid win.

No max-shard recommendation

Per instruction, not recommending a "maximum doable" count: the honest trend is that structure gets worse above ~0.5× (~55/29 shards), long before fabrication limits bind. The binding constraint here is fidelity, not fabrication — the opposite of what a "maximum doable" framing assumes.
