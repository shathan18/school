GIRL WITH A PEARL EARRING -- 3 views (3/4-front, frontal, 3/4-back), 60x60 grayscale multiplexing
===============================================================================================

Panels: 6 clear-perspex sheets, each 60x60 cm x 3mm, in a 2-family EGG-CRATE grid (3 sheets at
        60 deg + 3 at 150 deg, 13 cm pitch). Every intersection strictly 2-WAY (max 2).
        3 walls at 120 deg, 3 lights, 3x magnification, walls 1.8 m. ~0.12 m deep.
Shards: 588 -- light 373, medium 163, black 52. Min 5x5 mm.
Palette: clear + 3 dye tones (light/medium/black), stacking-additive across panels.

MULTIPLEXING: one fixed installation; light A -> 3/4-front, light B -> frontal, light C -> 3/4-back.
 Same shards build all three; grayscale reproduces the painting's tone. Backgrounds removed via
 colour-aware segmentation (neutral-dark bg vs brown jacket).

FABRICATION: cut_sheets/<panel>.svg or .dxf -- 3 layers (light/medium/black); laser-engrave shard
 outlines on a 60x60 clear sheet, hand-colour each region to its tone, mount at the panel azimuth.
