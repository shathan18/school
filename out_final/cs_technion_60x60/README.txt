CS x TECHNION -- 60x60 grayscale multiplexing build (latest method)
==================================================================

Panels: 4 clear-perspex sheets, each 60x60 cm x 3mm, 2-family EGG-CRATE grid (2 sheets at 60 deg
        + 2 at 150 deg). Every intersection strictly 2-WAY (max 2). 2 walls at 120 deg, 2 lights,
        3x magnification, walls 1.8 m. ~0.12 m deep.
Shards: 775 -- light 492, medium 215, black 68. Min 5x5 mm.
Palette: clear + 3 dye tones (light/medium/black), stacking-additive across panels.

MULTIPLEXING: one fixed installation; light A -> CS faculty mark, light B -> Technion emblem.
 Supersedes the earlier teammate build (which was sized for 2.0x1.6 m stock sheets).

FABRICATION: cut_sheets/<panel>.svg or .dxf -- 3 layers (light/medium/black); laser-engrave shard
 outlines on a 60x60 clear sheet, hand-colour each region to its tone, mount at the panel azimuth.
