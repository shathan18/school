GIRL WITH A PEARL EARRING -- front & back, 60x60 grayscale multiplexing build
===========================================================================

Panels: 4 clear-perspex sheets, each 60x60 cm x 3mm, in a 2-family EGG-CRATE grid (2 sheets at
        60 deg + 2 at 150 deg, 14 cm pitch). Every intersection is strictly 2-WAY (max 2).
        2 walls at 120 deg, 2 lights, 3x magnification, walls 1.8 m. ~0.12 m deep.
Shards: 684 -- light 391, medium 228, black 65. Min 5x5 mm.
Palette: clear + 3 dye tones (light/medium/black), stacking-additive across panels.

MULTIPLEXING: same fixed installation; light A shows the FRONT (face/turban/drape), light B shows
 the BACK (turned away, ponytail, dark dress). Grayscale reproduces the painting's tone.

FABRICATION: cut_sheets/<panel>.svg or .dxf -- 3 layers (light/medium/black); laser-engrave shard
 outlines on a 60x60 clear sheet, hand-colour each region to its tone, mount at the panel's azimuth.
